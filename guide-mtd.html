MTD for Income Tax

From 6 April 2026, sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000 must keep digital records and send quarterly updates. The threshold drops to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028. You need compatible software; a spreadsheet with bridging software is allowed.
