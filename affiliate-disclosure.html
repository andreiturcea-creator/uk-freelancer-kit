UK Freelancer Kit is free to use. To help pay for it, some links may be affiliate or referral links. If you click one and sign up or buy, we may earn a commission or credit, at no extra cost to you.

How we label them

  • Every affiliate or referral link is marked Affiliate link next to the link itself, not only on this page.
  • These links carry rel="sponsored" so search engines know they are paid links.
  • Our own product. The UK Sole Trader Kit (£9, sold on Gumroad) is made by us. Links to it are links to our own product, not affiliate links.

What it does not change

Commissions never change calculator results, figures, rankings or what we write. Tax figures come from GOV.UK. We only link to services we think are relevant, and you can always go to a provider directly instead.

Current partners

We list partner links only once a programme has approved us. Right now there are none.

This follows the UK CAP Code and ASA guidance that marketing must be obviously identifiable. See also our privacy notice.
