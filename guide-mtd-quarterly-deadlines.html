MTD quarterly update deadlines

Short answer: MTD for Income Tax quarterly updates are due by 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May. For 2026/27 that is 7 August 2026, 7 November 2026, 7 February 2027 and 7 May 2027.

You cannot switch between standard and calendar periods after sending a quarterly update for that year. Your return is still due by 31 January.

Frequently asked questions

What are the MTD quarterly update deadlines?

7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May (the following tax year). The deadlines are the same whether you use standard or calendar update periods.

What periods do the updates cover?

Standard periods are cumulative from 6 April: to 5 July, 5 October, 5 January and 5 April. Calendar periods run from 1 April and end on 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March.

What if I miss a quarterly deadline?

From 2027/28 each late update gets a penalty point, and 4 points brings a £200 penalty. HMRC will not apply penalty points for late quarterly updates in 2026/27.

Can I send updates early or more often?

Yes. You can send one any time between the end of the period and the deadline, and you can send more often as long as each update covers the full period from the start of the year.
