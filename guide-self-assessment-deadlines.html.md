# Self Assessment deadlines 2026/27: 31 January 2027 and more

Source: https://andreiturcea-creator.github.io/uk-freelancer-kit/guide-self-assessment-deadlines.html

**Short answer:** For the 2025/26 tax return: register by 5 October 2026, file a paper return by 31 October 2026, and file online and pay by 31 January 2027. The second payment on account is due by 31 July 2027.

| What | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Register for Self Assessment (new) | 5 October 2026 |
| Paper tax return | 31 October 2026 |
| Online tax return | 11:59pm, 31 January 2027 |
| Pay tax owed + 1st payment on account for 2026/27 | 31 January 2027 |
| 2nd payment on account | 31 July 2027 |

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the 2025/26 Self Assessment deadline?

Paper returns by 31 October 2026, online returns by 11:59pm on 31 January 2027, and any tax owed paid by 31 January 2027.

### When must I register for Self Assessment?

By 5 October after the end of the tax year you need to file for, for example 5 October 2026 for 2025/26.

### Is there a July payment?

Yes, if you make payments on account. The second payment is due by 31 July.

### I registered late. When is my deadline?

If you register after 5 October 2026, you must still pay what you owe by 31 January 2027.
