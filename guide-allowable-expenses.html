Allowable expenses for sole traders

Common categories: office costs, travel (not commuting), clothing (uniforms only), staff, stock, financial costs, premises, marketing, and training related to your current business. Instead of actual costs you can use simplified expenses: 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in 2026/27 (45p before 6 April 2026), then 25p, plus flat-rate working-from-home allowances. Or you can claim the £1,000 trading allowance instead of expenses.

FAQs

What expenses can sole traders claim?

Costs incurred wholly and exclusively for the business: office costs, business travel (not commuting), uniforms, staff, stock, financial costs, premises, marketing and training for your current business.

What is the trading allowance?

Your first £1,000 of self-employment income is tax-free. You can claim it instead of your actual expenses, but not both.

Can I use flat rates instead of actual costs?

Yes, simplified expenses cover vehicles (55p a mile for the first 10,000 miles in 2026/27), working from home and living at your business premises.
