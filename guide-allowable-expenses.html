UK Freelancer Kit

Allowable expenses

Common categories: office costs, travel (not commuting), clothing (uniforms only), staff, stock, financial costs, premises, marketing, training related to your current business. Instead of actual costs you can use simplified expenses: 55p/mile for the first 10,000 business miles (rate raised from 45p in June 2026), then 25p, and flat-rate working-from-home allowances. You can choose the £1,000 trading allowance instead of claiming expenses.

Source: GOV.UK, 'Expenses if you're self-employed'.
